## Notes
- Format: one `explain` step, ids `oz{n}q{k}`, group constants `OZG1` (from 1), later `OZG2…` per group. Lint also rejects person/body emojis (faces, hands, people).

- Lesson 2: the I. Karimov quotation (7-mashq) not used.
- Lesson 14: Navoiy 26 000+ / Pushkin 21 000+ word-stock figures kept as the book states them («mutaxassislar taʼkidlaydi»).
- Lesson 17: no invented chart — a rasmiy → ilmiy → badiiy chain instead of bars.
- Each lesson ends with 1–2 of the book's frazeologik ibora definitions as a question.
- Ona tili 2: the four «Matn va lugʻat bilan ishlash» lessons (4, 7, 12, 20) and Mustahkamlash/Nazorat ishi skipped.
