## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
QuvnoqTest.uz is a gamified, quiz-based learning web application for Uzbekistan school students in grades 1–11. It delivers short, interactive "quiz lessons" mapped to the national curriculum. A **free tier** exposes demo lessons; a **paid tier** (15 000 UZS / month) unlocks full content plus an AI tutor.

### 1.2 Scope of this release (v1.0)
This release builds:
- The full **public shell**: landing page, grade/subject taxonomy navigation, free dashboard, paid dashboard, profile, upgrade, dummy checkout, and an AI tutor.
- **One fully-built quiz lesson** — Grade 1 (1-sinf) → Mathematics (Matematika) → Topic 1 (*Narsalarning xossalari*) — generated by the agent from the supplied book (see §9).
- **40 dummy quiz pages** — Grade 1 Mathematics topics 2–41 — each showing its real topic title and a "Coming soon" body.
- **All other subjects and all other grades** as clickable stubs that route to a subject page reading "🚧 Coming soon …".

### 1.3 Definitions
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Quiz lesson** | A single topic delivered as a recall + 10 interactive test items + completion screen. |
| **Demo / sinov darsi** | A free quiz lesson. In free tier, every lesson is labelled "demo classes" (UZ: *sinov darslar*). |
| **Silent registration** | Anonymous user record auto-created during the demo, before any email is given. |
| **Magic link** | Passwordless email login used to claim/resume an account on any device. |
| **`paid_until`** | Timestamp marking when paid access expires. Paid status = `paid_until > now()`. |
| **Recall** | Brief, kid-friendly explanation of the topic shown before the tests. |

## 2. Product Overview

### 2.1 Vision
A delightful, low-friction place where a child (or parent) can pick a grade, pick a subject, and immediately *play* a learning quiz — no signup wall. Value is demonstrated first; registration and payment come later.

### 2.2 Tiers
| | Free | Paid (15 000 UZS / month) |
|---|---|---|
| Demo lesson (Topic 1) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Full lessons (Topics 2–41 + other subjects) | 🔒 → Upgrade | ✅ (dummy "Coming soon" pages in v1.0) |
| AI tutor | 🔒 → Upgrade | ✅ (5 msgs/day, 1000 tokens/day) |
| Progress saved | ✅ | ✅ |

### 2.3 Tier lifecycle (critical)
- Clicking **"I PAID"** on the dummy checkout sets `paid_until = now() + 30 days` and flips the user to the paid experience.
- On every app load the app checks `paid_until > now()`. When it lapses, the user **reverts to free**:
  - They can again open **only** the demo lesson (Topic 1).
  - Their learned/not-learned lesson history **remains visible** on the dashboard but locked items become paywalled again.
  - The **AI tutor section** stays on the dashboard but becomes paywalled again.

### 2.4 Languages
Trilingual: **Uzbek (default), Russian, English.** Both **UI chrome and quiz content** must be fully translated (see §8 and §9).

### 5.3 Typography
- Friendly, rounded sans-serif with full **Latin + Cyrillic** coverage (Uzbek Latin uses `oʻ gʻ ' ` — pick a font that renders the modifier letters correctly). Recommended: **Nunito** or **Baloo 2** (kid-friendly, both scripts).
- **Dashboard headline rule (hard requirement):** the "What are we learning today?" headline is **3rem (48px)**, top-center.

### 5.4 Tone of motion
Playful but performant: confetti on success, gentle bounce on selection, smooth page transitions. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (when set, skip the entrance choreography and cross-fades, show final state directly).

### 5.5 Grade-tile illustration system (educational sketches)
Grade tiles must **not** be plain book stacks. Each tile is a stack-of-books-from-above motif **decorated with hand-drawn-style educational sketches appropriate to that grade's level**, so a child reads the grade's "feel" at a glance:
- **Grades 1–2 (early):** counting dots, simple shapes (○ △ □), numbers 1–10, an apple/pencil, a sun.
- **Grades 3–4:** multiplication dots, a ruler, simple fractions (½), a leaf/plant, a clock.
- **Grades 5–6:** basic algebra hints (x, y), a globe/map, a beaker, a music note.
- **Grades 7–9:** formula sketches (a²+b², triangle with angles), atom/molecule, circuit symbol, chemistry flask.
- **Grades 10–11:** integral/derivative marks (∫, f′(x)), DNA helix, physics vectors, an astronomy planet/telescope.
Use a consistent **chalk/marker sketch style** (royalty-free or generated; no copyrighted artwork). Keep sketches lightweight (SVG preferred) for performance.

### 5.6 Subject visuals & book mascots
- **Subject tiles** must each carry a **relevant science/subject sketch** (not a plain label): e.g. Matematika → shapes + numbers; Fizika → atom/pendulum; Kimyo → flask; Biologiya → leaf/cell; Geografiya → globe; Ingliz tili → ABC/speech bubble; Musiqa → note; Tasviriy sanʼat → palette; etc. Same sketch style as §5.5.
- **Subject book mascots:** each subject has its own friendly **"book character" mascot** — an illustrated open/standing book with a face — themed to the subject (the **Matematika** mascot wears math motifs: numbers/shapes). The mascot is the app's companion *inside* a subject:
  - It is the **read-aloud control** on the topic-explanation block (a large mascot with a big **play button** on it — see §7.4), replacing any plain "Listen/Tinglash" button.
  - It appears on **every page within that subject** (lesson, dashboard subject view, dummy "Coming soon" pages) as a small persistent companion (§7.6).
  - Provide mascots as SVG/PNG in `/public/mascots/<subject_key>.*`. At minimum, ship the **Matematika** mascot fully; other subjects may reuse a templated book mascot recolored/re-iconed per subject until bespoke art exists.

## 6. Functional Requirements — Pages & Flows

> Requirement IDs use `FR-<area>-<n>`. Each is testable.

### 6.1 Landing page (first-time "wow")
- **FR-LND-1** Default language **Uzbek**; language switcher top-right (UZ / RU / EN) persists choice.
- **FR-LND-2 Intro choreography (exact):** on first paint, show the **animated logo centered**, **nothing else** (no grids, no nav, no background clutter). Then the **logo animates upward** to its header/top position, and **only after that** the **grade tiles fade/slide in slowly**. No other elements appear during the intro. The language switcher may appear with/after the logo settles. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by showing the settled state immediately. *(This sequence supersedes the earlier generic "animated hero" wording.)*
- **FR-LND-3 Grade tiles:** grades render as a **grid, 3 per row**, each a **stack-of-books-from-above** motif **decorated with grade-appropriate educational sketches** per §5.5 (so tiles are visually rich, not plain). 
- **FR-LND-4** Grades 1-sinf … 11-sinf in order. **All grade tiles except `1-sinf` show a 🚧 badge over the name.**
- **FR-LND-5 Subjects expand from inside the grade:** clicking a grade **opens that grade's subjects out of the tile itself** (e.g. the book "opens"/expands and the subjects emerge from within) — **do not** render a separate subjects list at the bottom of the page. Subjects animate in from the grade. Each **subject tile carries its subject science sketch** (§5.6) and shows a 🚧 badge over its name **except `1-sinf → Matematika`**.
- **FR-LND-5a Subject open animation:** clicking a subject opens it **with animation**, reusing the subject's science sketch/mascot as the transition motif (e.g. the sketch scales up / the book mascot leads into the page).
- **FR-LND-6** Clicking **1-sinf → Matematika** starts the **demo quiz lesson** (Topic 1).
- **FR-LND-7** Clicking any **other** subject (any grade) routes to that subject's page showing **"🚧 Coming soon …"** (§7.3).
- **FR-LND-8** Starting the demo triggers **silent registration** (§6.6.1).

### 6.2 Demo quiz lesson — the core experience
Flow: **Recall → 10 test items (one at a time) → Completion**.

- **FR-QZ-1 Recall (with mascot read-aloud):** show the paraphrased topic title with relevant emojis and a brief, kid-friendly explanation (generated per §9). The **read-aloud control for this block is the large Matematika book mascot with a big play button on it** (§5.6, §7.4) — tapping it narrates the **topic title + explanation paragraph** via TTS. This is the **only** place audio is offered.
- **FR-QZ-2 Test items appear one by one** (not all on one screen), with a progress indicator (e.g. 3/10).
- **FR-QZ-3 Item variety & engagement:** the 10 items must feel **fun and varied** — span multiple mechanics (single/multi click-correct, **drag-and-drop**, matching/connect-the-line, sequence/pattern continuation, sorting, and at least one **creative/interactive** item), each with playful motion, colorful shape/icon assets, and satisfying feedback (§7.5). Avoid repeating the same mechanic back-to-back. **The agent selects each item's mechanic based on the book's exercises** (§9.4).
- **FR-QZ-4 Feedback & effects:** immediate visual + sound feedback on answer (correct = green/chime/confetti; incorrect = gentle shake/soft tone, no harsh negativity). All sounds royalty-free (§7.5).
- **FR-QZ-5 Help (Yordam) button:** persistent **"Help / Yordam"** control with a **dropdown / expander** giving a **hint that helps the child understand the question better** (re-explains what to do in simpler words, generated per §9). For interactive/action items (drag, match, sort, sequence) the hint also includes a **small visual cue** — e.g. a faint arrow showing "drag here," a pulsing target/drop-zone outline, a finger/hand icon, or one example pairing — so younger users understand the interaction. Hints are per-item.
- **FR-QZ-6 (removed)** No per-question read-aloud. Audio is limited to the recall block (FR-QZ-1) to minimise TTS cost.
- **FR-QZ-7 Scoring:** track correct/incorrect per item and a total score.
- **FR-QZ-8 Completion screen:** show **"Congratulations!"**, the **score**, a **large "Go to Dashboard" button**, then a **per-item review**: for each of the 10 items, show whether it was correct/incorrect and a short **explanation of why** (the explanation text is generated per §9).
- **FR-QZ-9 Trilingual:** all recall, prompts, options, hints (incl. visual-cue labels), feedback, and explanations available in UZ/RU/EN, following the active language.
- **FR-QZ-10 Persistence:** on completion, mark Topic 1 as completed for this user with the score (§12).
- **FR-QZ-11 Touch + mouse:** drag-and-drop and all interactions must work with both touch and pointer (mobile + desktop).
- **FR-QZ-12 In-lesson navigation:** every lesson screen shows clear **Back** (previous step) and **To Dashboard** controls (§6.10), in addition to the large "Go to Dashboard" button on completion.

### 6.6 Profile menu & registration
- **FR-PRF-1 Identity display:** show the system-generated **ID** (e.g. `QT-48213`) — **visible to the user**.
- **FR-PRF-2 Grade:** known from landing selection; **editable** here.
- **FR-PRF-3 Optional details:** name, phone number, email — **all optional**, editable.

#### 6.6.1 Silent registration flow
1. Visitor starts the demo (FR-LND-6/8).
2. System creates a user record with a generated public ID and stores the chosen grade + locale. No email yet.
3. The record is tied to the browser via a durable token (HTTP-only cookie + localStorage fallback) so the same device resumes automatically.

#### 6.6.2 Claim-by-email (magic link)
1. User enters email in profile.
2. Backend sends a magic link to that email.
3. On click, the link verifies and **attaches the email to the existing silent record** (play-first, register-later; progress carries over). If that email already has an account, define a documented merge/cutover rule (recommended: keep the email's canonical account and migrate device progress into it; never silently destroy progress).

### 6.9 AI tutor (paid)
- **FR-AI-1 Model:** Claude **Haiku** (`claude-haiku-4-5`) via a **server-side** endpoint. The API key is never exposed to the client.
- **FR-AI-2 Role:** **general tutor** for the student (age-appropriate, encouraging, curriculum-relevant help). Provide a system prompt that keeps answers short, kind, and suitable for young learners; respond in the user's active language.
- **FR-AI-3 Access:** paid users only. Free users who open it are routed to Upgrade (§6.7).
- **FR-AI-4 Daily limits (per user, per calendar day):**
  - **5 messages/day** (counts user messages / full exchanges).
  - **1000 tokens/day**, **input + output combined**.
- **FR-AI-5 Pre-send gating:** **before sending**, if the new exchange would exceed the remaining daily token budget, **block the message** (do not cut a response off mid-stream). Likewise block once 5 messages are used.
- **FR-AI-6 Usage display:** the AI panel shows **current usage vs limit** (e.g. "3 / 5 messages today · 420 / 1000 tokens").
- **FR-AI-7 Limit-reached state:** show a **"limit reached"** notice; the user **cannot use the AI** until the next day, but **everything else in the app keeps working**.
- **FR-AI-8 Reset:** limits reset daily (define the timezone explicitly — recommend Asia/Tashkent).

### 6.10 Global navigation
- **FR-NAV-1** Every page below the dashboard provides a clear, consistent **Back** control (returns to the previous screen) **and** a **To Dashboard** control. Applies to: the demo lesson (all steps), the per-item review, stub "Coming soon" pages, the 40 dummy topic pages, the Upgrade page, and the dummy checkout.
- **FR-NAV-2** Controls use clear icons + localized labels (UZ/RU/EN), large enough for children and reachable with a thumb on mobile (place within easy reach, e.g. top-left Back, and a visible To-Dashboard action).
- **FR-NAV-3** Back never traps the user; from any dead-end (e.g. a stub page) To Dashboard always returns to the dashboard for the user's grade.

### 7.2 🚧 Badge rule
Render a 🚧 badge **over the name** of every grade tile and every subject tile **except** `1-sinf` (grade) and its `Matematika` (subject).

### 7.3 Stub subject page
Any stubbed subject (and any subject opened on a non-1-sinf grade) routes to a page reading **"🚧 Coming soon …"** (localized). The page shows that subject's **book mascot** (§5.6) and consistent **Back** + **To Dashboard** controls (§6.10).

### 7.4 Read-aloud (TTS) — topic explanation only
- **FR-TTS-1 (scope, cost-critical):** read-aloud is offered **only on the topic-explanation block** of a built lesson — i.e. the **topic title + the short explanation paragraph** (the recall). **No** read-aloud on questions, options, hints, dashboards, or anywhere else. This deliberately minimises TTS usage.
- **FR-TTS-2 Control = mascot:** the play control is the **large subject book mascot with a big play button on it** (§5.6) — not a plain "Listen/Tinglash" button. Tapping the mascot plays the explanation audio.
- **FR-TTS-3 Provider & settings (fixed):** use the Google **Gemini TTS** API with credentials from `.env` (already provided by sponsor).
  - **Model:** `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`
  - **Voice:** **Charon** (male)
  - Tone/style configurable; choose a warm, clear delivery suited to young children. *(Treat the model/voice IDs as the sponsor's stated preference; if the exact preview model string is unavailable at build time, flag it and use the nearest current Gemini TTS model with the Charon voice, then confirm.)*
- **FR-TTS-4 Caching (hard requirement) + "save as much as possible":** because the recall text is fixed/hardcoded, synthesize each language's clip **once** via a one-time **seed step**, store it (Supabase Storage / static asset) keyed by hash of `locale + voice + model + text`, and **always serve from cache** thereafter. Never synthesize on page view. Total synthesis = **3 short clips** (UZ/RU/EN) for the one built lesson → effectively $0.

## 13. Technical Stack (sized to $0–$20 / month, 100 concurrent)

> Rationale: maximize managed **free tiers**; keep the only usage-billed pieces (AI, TTS) tightly capped/cached.

### 13.1 Frontend
- **Next.js (React, App Router)** + **TypeScript**. Responsive (mobile + desktop).
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with the brand palette as CSS variables (§5.2).
- **i18n:** `next-intl` (or `next-i18next`) with `uz/ru/en` message files.
- **Animation:** Framer Motion + CSS; confetti via a lightweight lib (e.g. `canvas-confetti`).
- **Drag-and-drop:** `dnd-kit` (touch- and pointer-friendly).
- **Drawing pen:** HTML5 `<canvas>` (pointer events; no heavy dependency).
- **Art assets:** grade/subject sketches (§5.5) and subject **book mascots** (§5.6) as **SVG** (preferred) in `/public/sketches/` and `/public/mascots/`; keep lightweight for mobile.
- **Sound:** `howler.js` playing cached royalty-free assets; global mute.

### 13.2 Backend
- **Next.js Route Handlers** provide the **server-side backend tier** (this satisfies the "traditional backend" requirement — these are real server endpoints, not client code). They host: AI tutor proxy, TTS synthesis/seed + cache lookup, magic-link issuance/verification, paid-status mutation, and progress APIs.
- **Alternative (if you prefer a separate service):** a small **Node + Fastify/Express** (or NestJS) service on a free-tier host (Railway/Render/Fly.io). Use this only if strict frontend/backend separation is desired; it adds ops overhead and is not required to meet the budget.

### 13.3 Database, Auth & Storage
- **Supabase (free tier)** is the recommended single provider:
  - **Postgres** for all tables in §12.
  - **Supabase Auth** for **email magic links** (native) — covers §6.6.2.
  - **Supabase Storage** for **cached TTS audio** and static media.
- Free tier comfortably covers 100 concurrent users and this data volume. (Alternatives: Neon/Railway Postgres + a separate mailer for magic links — more moving parts.)

### 13.4 External services
- **Google Gemini TTS** for narration of the **recall block only** (§7.4). Model `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, voice **Charon** (male). Credentials from `.env` (server-side only). **Cache the 3 clips** from a one-time seed script — never synthesize per request.
- **Anthropic Claude API** — model **`claude-haiku-4-5`** for the AI tutor (§6.9). Server-side key. Enforce per-user daily caps in `ai_usage` **before** sending (FR-AI-5).

### 13.5 Cost model (why this stays ≤ $20/month)
- **Hosting (Vercel Hobby):** $0.
- **Supabase free tier (DB + Auth + Storage):** $0.
- **Gemini TTS:** read-aloud is **recall-only** and **cached** — exactly **3 short clips** (UZ/RU/EN) synthesized once for the one built lesson → **≈ $0**. *(Verify current Gemini TTS pricing/free allowance at build time; the recall-only scope keeps this negligible regardless.)*
- **Claude Haiku** (only real variable cost) at **$1 / M input, $5 / M output** (verified June 2026):
  - Hard cap = **1000 tokens/user/day**. Worst case 100 daily-active users maxing out: 100 × 1000 × 30 = **3 M tokens/month**.
  - Even if **all** were output tokens: 3 M × $5/M = **$15/month** (absolute ceiling).
  - Realistic split (≈600 in / 400 out): ≈ **$7–8/month**.
- **Total worst case ≈ $15/month**, within the $20 ceiling. The **daily token cap is the cost governor** — do not remove it.

### 13.6 Configuration & content
- Content (taxonomy, the built Topic-1 lesson, the 40 dummy titles) lives in **versioned TS/JSON** under `content/` and locale files under `messages/`. No CMS in v1.0.
- Secrets via environment variables; never committed.
